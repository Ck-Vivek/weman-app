Pro Form Cues

Coaching directives to protect spinal alignment

verified

Keep your pelvis tucked into a posterior tilt during all rollouts and leg raises. Eliminate any hyperextension (arching) in your lumbar spine to direct full tension strictly into the rectus abdominis.

Breathe through the diaphragm. Exhale forcefully through pursed lips at peak concentric contraction (top of hanging raise, full roll extension) to engage the deep transverse abdominis sheath.

During single-arm plank reaches and woodchoppers, lock headlights on your hip bones facing directly toward the floor/anchor. Resisting rotational torso sway is where true stability is earned.
